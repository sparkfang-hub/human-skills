# Code of conduct

Human Skills is about helping agents respond more carefully to human vulnerability. Contributors are expected to show the same care to one another.

Be respectful, specific, and open to correction. Critique wording and behavior, not the person proposing it. Do not use contributions to promote harassment, manipulation, discrimination, humiliation, medical misinformation, or exploitation of people in distress.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that undermines user safety or a constructive working environment. Serious or repeated concerns may be reported through the repository's moderation and security channels.
