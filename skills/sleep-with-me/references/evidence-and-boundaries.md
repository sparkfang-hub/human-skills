# Evidence and boundaries

This reference explains the conservative public guidance behind `sleep-with-me`. It is not a treatment manual.

## Why the skill stays small

The American Academy of Sleep Medicine describes cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia, usually delivered over multiple sessions, as first-line evidence-based treatment for chronic insomnia. It combines several components rather than a single bedtime trick.

That is why this skill does not claim to deliver CBT-I and does not prescribe a personalized sleep-restriction program.

Sources:

- AASM, "Coding Quarterly: Cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia": https://aasm.org/coding-quarterly-cognitive-behavioral-therapy-for-insomnia/
- AASM, "New guideline supports behavioral, psychological treatments for insomnia": https://aasm.org/new-guideline-supports-behavioral-psychological-treatments-for-insomnia/

## Do not force sleep

NHS public guidance advises not trying to force sleep. When wakefulness has become frustrating, it suggests getting up for a relaxing activity and returning to bed when sleepier.

Source:

- NHS Every Mind Matters, "Fall asleep faster and sleep better": https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/how-to-fall-asleep-faster-and-sleep-better/

The skill presents this as an option, not a rigid rule or timed performance target.

## Conditions that need more care

The US Department of Veterans Affairs describes Insomnia Coach as a self-care tool based on CBT-I, while warning users to consult a health professional before changing sleep habits when certain conditions may be present, including sleep apnea, restless legs, bipolar disorder, seizure disorders, sleepwalking, night terrors, excessive daytime sleepiness, and fall risk.

Source:

- VA Insomnia Coach: https://mobile.va.gov/app/insomnia-coach

The skill does not diagnose these conditions. It only recognizes that a generic bedtime protocol may be inappropriate and suggests professional assessment.

## Crisis boundary

A sleep conversation is not an emergency service. When the user cannot keep themselves or someone else safe, the normal flow stops and local emergency or crisis support takes priority.

For the United States and its territories:

- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: https://988lifeline.org/

For other locations, use current local emergency or crisis resources rather than assuming a country.

## What the skill intentionally excludes

- medication or supplement changes;
- alcohol, cannabis, or sedative advice;
- personalized sleep restriction;
- treatment claims;
- diagnosis;
- promises to induce sleep;
- simulated physical or romantic companionship.
